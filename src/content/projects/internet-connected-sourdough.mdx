Proof-of-concept made for the
"[Creative Technology](http://iis-internetdagarna-arkiv.s3-website.eu-north-1.amazonaws.com/arkiv/2016/index.html%3Fp=18672.html)"

[Peder Fjällström](https://twitter.com/fjallstrom).
