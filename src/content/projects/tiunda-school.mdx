Anton Alvarez, himself a former student at Tiunda, was invited to ornament the
entrances of the school and its preschool. What came of it was a ten metre high
clay press, built to produce sixteen concrete pillars.

With mechanical, electronic and software engineering I helped Anton construct
and animate the machine that pressed them: a custom control board, four drives
in a cabinet, and a handheld radio remote to run the press from the ground.
