[Puratos](https://www.puratos.com/), a global company known for its work in
bakery, patisserie, and chocolate, teamed up with me on a project to build
Sourbot — an automated sourdough robot designed for research and development. It
helps start and grow small-batch sourdough cultures from scratch, tracks how
they're developing, and shares that data via a simple dashboard and API.

Puratos also runs the world-renowned [Sourdough
Library](https://sourdoughlibrary.puratos.com/en/), a collection of sourdough
cultures from around the world. It holds over 120 active sourdoughs, more than
1400 strains of wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria, and hosts a library of
3000+ open-source recipes online.

The project was a close collaboration with researchers and data scientists at
Puratos. I designed and built the complete robot, including hardware,
electronics and software that integrated into existing systems via a cloud API.

The project was built at the hardware innovation and design lab
[Toolspace](https://www.toolspace.se/) in Stockholm. Also check out the
[proof of concept](/internet-connected-sourdough) that inspired the creation of
this machine.
