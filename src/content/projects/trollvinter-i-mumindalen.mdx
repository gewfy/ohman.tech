An immersive children’s exhibition at
[Junibacken](https://www.junibacken.se/upplevelse/trollvinter-i-mumindalen/),
based on Tove Jansson’s Moomin book _Trollvinter_ (1957).

I contributed to the interaction design and built custom inductive LED candles for the snow lanterns: no wires, no contacts, no visible power. I also set up lidar tracking that let visitors interact with projections by throwing snowballs or touching the walls.

The exhibition was produced in collaboration with HHNW Studio, [Null Division](https://nulldi.vision/) and [Rights & Brands Moomin](https://rightsandbrands.com/brands/moomin/).
