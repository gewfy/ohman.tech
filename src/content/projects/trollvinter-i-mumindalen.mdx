An immersive children’s exhibition at
[Junibacken](https://www.junibacken.se/upplevelse/trollvinter-i-mumindalen/),
based on Tove Jansson’s _Trollvinter_. I helped with inductive LED candles for
the snow lanterns and lidar-enabled projected wall interactions — electronics
design, parts manufacturing and sourcing.

The exhibition was produced in collaboration with Null Division, Moomin Characters and
Rights & Brands.
